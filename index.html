Community within, hope beyond

Two halves of one collective

Academia asks a lot of the body and of time: late nights, long travel, unforgiving schedules, intense pressure and stress. Illness, disability, and isolation can quietly push people out of a field they love. What if we could hold on to those people? And together, we carry the sky to others who have been shut out of it?

Within

A supportive network for astronomers facing difficult circumstances: chronic illness, disability, caregiving, grief, or the isolation that comes with any of them.

  • Peer support groups that meet online
  • Seminars and workshops on navigating academia through difficulty
  • Practical guidance on accommodations, funding, and conference access
  • Mentorship pairs, matched by field and by circumstance
  • A shared library of accessible career and professional development resources

Beyond

Outreach training and opportunitues for members who want to bring astronomy to people the field rarely reaches. Outreach sessions for those isolated from society.

  • Outreach training built around real venue constraints
  • Session kits for indoor, low-mobility, and no-telescope settings
  • Partner introductions and safeguarding guidance
  • Ongoing support from members who've run sessions before

Where we go

We work with places where people are cut off from the outside world, sometimes for weeks, sometimes for years. Every session is designed for the room it happens in.

Hospitals

Bedside sessions, ward talks, and ceiling projections or virtual reality headsets for patients who can't get to a window, let alone a dark sky.

Care homes

Evening gatherings built around memory and recognition: the constellations residents grew up naming, and the missions they watched live.

Prisons

Structured astronomy sessions, courses, and instruction for citizen science projects, offering a way to look far past the walls.

Hospices and home care

Quiet, unhurried sessions for people who can't leave a room, and for the families sitting with them.

Who we are

A collective built on mutual care.

The Same Stars Collective is a community of students, professionals, and amateur astronomers who look after each other.

We support astronomers navigating illness, disability, and isolation offering the connection and backing that hardship can strip away. Together, we then carry the wonder of the night sky to people cut off from the world, holding stargazing sessions and astronomy experiences in hospitals, care homes, and prisons.

We believe that whoever and wherever we are, we all look up at the same stars and that no one should have to face them alone.

Founded by M. C. Davis in 2026.
